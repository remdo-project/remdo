RemDo

Keyboard-first collaborative outliner.

RemDo is an open-source workspace for people whose work depends on information spread across many tools. Start with whatever part you remember and follow its connections to related information.

Today, RemDo is a keyboard-first collaborative outliner: structure information, link notes and documents, search within documents, edit together in real time, and work offline.

Fully open source and designed to keep self-hosting a first-class option.

  • Keyboard-first outlining
  • Links between notes and documents
  • Real-time collaboration
  • Works offline
