RemDo is an open-source workspace for keeping information connected. It starts with a keyboard-first collaborative outliner: a place to organize notes, follow links between documents, and work through ideas together.

Today, you can structure notes, search within documents, edit together in real time, and work offline. RemDo is still early. The outliner is the starting point for the wider workspace we’re building.

Our vision is to connect the context scattered across your tools, from meeting notes to emails and calendars. Those connections are still in development, with privacy and the option to self-host guiding the work.
