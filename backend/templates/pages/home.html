RemDo is an open-source workspace for keeping information connected. It starts with a keyboard-first collaborative outliner: a place to organize notes, follow links between documents, and work through ideas together.

Today, you can structure notes, search within documents, edit together in real time, and work offline. RemDo is still early. The outliner is the starting point for the wider workspace we’re building.

Our vision is to connect the context scattered across your tools, from meeting notes to emails and calendars. Those connections are still in development, with privacy and the option to self-host guiding the work.

Frequently asked questions.

Is RemDo still in early development?

Yes. The core outliner is available, while the wider workspace is still taking shape. Features, the interface, and how the product works may change as development continues.

What can I do with RemDo today?

Organize notes in an outline, link notes and documents, and search within a document. You can also edit together in real time and work offline.

Can I use RemDo offline?

Yes, if you have a remembered session and the document is already stored on your device. You can keep editing and searching it; local changes sync when you reconnect.

Can I work with other people?

Yes. Document owners can share with other accounts on the same RemDo server, then edit together in real time. Sending someone a document link alone does not give them access.

Is RemDo open source?

Yes. RemDo is available under the MIT License, and you can inspect the code on GitHub. Self-hosting is supported if you want to run your own instance.

Does RemDo connect to email and calendars?

Not yet. Connections to email, calendars, and external files are part of the longer-term vision and are still under development. Today’s product focuses on RemDo notes and documents.
