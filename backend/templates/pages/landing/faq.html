Frequently asked questions.

Is RemDo still in early development?

Yes. The outliner works today; connections to other tools are still being built, and features and the interface may change.

Can I use RemDo offline?

Yes, if you have a remembered session and the document is already stored on your device. You can keep editing and searching it; local changes sync when you reconnect.

Can I work with other people?

Yes. Document owners can share with other accounts on the same RemDo server, then edit together in real time. Sending someone a document link alone does not give them access.

Is RemDo open source?

Yes. RemDo is available under the MIT License, and you can inspect the code on GitHub. Self-hosting is supported if you want to run your own instance.

Can I use RemDo with an AI assistant?

Yes. Claude, ChatGPT and other assistants that support the open MCP standard can read and add to your documents once you approve access, and you can revoke it any time.
