Frequently asked questions

Is RemDo still in early development?

Yes. You can use the core outliner now. The wider workspace is still in development. Features and the interface may change.

What can I do with RemDo today?

You can organize notes in an outline, link notes and documents, and search within a document. You can also edit documents with other people in real time and work offline.

Can I use RemDo offline?

Yes, if RemDo remembers your session and the document is stored on your device. You can continue to edit and search the document. RemDo syncs your changes when you reconnect.

Can I work with other people?

Yes. The document owner can share the document with other users on the same RemDo server. Users with access can edit the document in real time. A document link does not give access.

Does RemDo connect to email and calendars?

Not yet. Connections to email, calendars, and external files are still in development. Today, RemDo works with notes and documents created in RemDo.
