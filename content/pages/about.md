---
title: About RemDo
description: A keyboard-first collaborative outliner.
---

RemDo is an open-source workspace for people whose work depends on information
spread across many tools. A useful detail might be in meeting notes, an email
thread, a calendar invitation, or a shared document. Finding one piece often
still means reconstructing its context.

We’re building RemDo so you can start with whatever part you remember and follow
its connections to related information. A meeting could connect directly to the
notes taken during it, the email conversation that followed, and the document
the team was discussing. You could also create connections of your own without
moving everything into another system.

Today, RemDo is a keyboard-first collaborative outliner for organizing and
connecting notes. You can structure information, link notes and documents,
search within documents, edit together in real time, and work offline. The
broader cross-tool model, including email, calendars, and external files, is
still under development.

RemDo is privacy-first, fully open source, and designed to keep self-hosting a
first-class option. You can inspect the code and choose where your data lives.
As we connect more sources, respecting their privacy and access boundaries
remains a guiding principle.
