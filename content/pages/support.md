For questions, bug reports, and feature requests, please
[search existing GitHub issues or open a new one](https://github.com/remdo-project/remdo/issues).

For security vulnerabilities, privacy requests, or account problems involving
personal information, email [piotr@remdo.com](mailto:piotr@remdo.com). Please don’t
post sensitive information in public issues.
