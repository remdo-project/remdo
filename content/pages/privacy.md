_Last updated: 23 September 2026._

RemDo is an early-stage project under active development. This Privacy Policy
explains how personal data is handled when you use the RemDo service operated at
remdo.com.

The service is currently operated by **Piotr Lewalski**, a private individual
based in Poland, who is the controller of personal data described in this
policy.

For privacy questions or requests, contact
**[piotr@remdo.com](mailto:piotr@remdo.com)**.

## Information we process

Depending on how you use RemDo, we process:

- **Account information**, such as your email address, name, account identifier
  and authentication information.
- **Information from an authentication provider** if you choose to sign in
  through a third-party service. This can include an account identifier, name,
  email address or profile information, depending on the provider and
  permissions involved.
- **Content you provide to RemDo**, such as documents, notes and other
  information you choose to store or share.
- **Technical and security information**, such as IP address, browser or device
  information, authentication events and server logs needed to operate, secure
  and troubleshoot the service.
- **Usage information**, if you consent to optional analytics, such as features
  used, interactions with the service and information used to understand usage
  patterns or run product experiments such as A/B tests.
- **Information you provide when contacting us**, for example when requesting
  support or providing feedback.

## Why we process information

The purposes and legal bases for processing are:

- **Creating and managing your account; authenticating you; storing and
  synchronizing your content; providing sharing, collaboration and integrations
  that you request; and sending necessary account or service communications.**
  Legal basis: performance of our agreement with you to provide RemDo.
- **Operating and protecting the service, maintaining reliability, diagnosing
  failures, preventing abuse and investigating security incidents.** Legal
  basis: our legitimate interests in operating a secure, reliable service and
  protecting RemDo and its users.
- **Responding to support requests and feedback.** Legal basis: performance of
  the service where the request relates to your account or use of RemDo, and
  otherwise our legitimate interest in communicating with users and improving
  the service.
- **Optional product analytics and experiments, including A/B tests.** Legal
  basis: your consent.
- **Processing required to comply with applicable law or binding legal
  requirements.** Legal basis: legal obligation.

Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time.
Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before
consent was withdrawn.

## Information required to use RemDo

You are not legally required to provide personal data to RemDo.

However, some information is necessary to provide particular features. For
example, account information is required to create and maintain an account, and
information from an authentication provider is required if you choose that
method of signing in.

Content, integrations and other information are provided when you choose to use
the corresponding RemDo features.

Optional analytics is not required to use the core RemDo service.

## Analytics and product experiments

RemDo may use optional analytics to understand how the product is used and
improve it. This can include measuring feature usage, usage patterns and
retention, and testing different versions of features through experiments such
as A/B tests.

Where analytics or experimentation involves non-essential cookies, browser
storage or similar technologies, it is enabled only after you have given consent
where consent is required.

Declining optional analytics does not prevent you from using the core RemDo
service.

RemDo does not use personal data for advertising and does not use the contents
of your documents for advertising.

## Cookies and browser storage

RemDo uses browser storage needed to provide the service.

This includes local browser storage used for account-related state and
**IndexedDB and local storage used for local and offline document
functionality**. Locally persisted document data is encrypted by RemDo before
being stored in the browser.

Authentication methods may also use cookies or similar browser mechanisms
necessary to keep you signed in and secure your session.

These technologies are used where necessary to provide functionality you request
and do not depend on optional analytics consent.

Any additional browser storage used specifically for optional analytics or
product experimentation is subject to the analytics consent described above
where required by law.

## Authentication

RemDo can support authentication using an email address and password or through
third-party authentication providers.

If you choose a third-party provider, RemDo receives information needed to
identify your account, such as an account identifier, name or email address,
depending on that provider and the permissions involved.

The authentication provider separately processes information under its own
privacy policy.

Passwords used directly with RemDo are not stored in readable form.

## Your content and third-party integrations

RemDo processes content you provide so that it can provide the functionality you
request.

You can also choose to give another service or application access to information
in RemDo, for example through an integration, API or MCP connection.

When you deliberately authorize such an integration, RemDo can provide
information requested through that connection to the service you authorized. How
that third party subsequently processes the information is governed by its own
terms and privacy practices.

RemDo does not provide your document content to third-party integrations unless
the functionality you use requires it or you authorize the connection.

When you deliberately share or collaborate on content through RemDo, information
associated with that content or collaboration may be visible to the people you
share it with.

## Service providers and other recipients

We use third-party providers where necessary to operate RemDo. These can include
providers of:

- infrastructure, hosting and databases;
- authentication;
- email delivery;
- monitoring, security and error reporting;
- analytics and product experimentation, where enabled.

These providers process personal data only to the extent relevant to the
services they provide.

Personal data can also be disclosed:

- to people with whom you deliberately share or collaborate;
- to services you choose to connect to RemDo;
- where disclosure is required by law; or
- where reasonably necessary to protect users, RemDo or the security and
  integrity of the service.

We do not sell personal data.

## International processing

Some service providers can process personal data outside Poland or the European
Economic Area.

Where GDPR requires safeguards for such a transfer, we rely on an applicable
transfer mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or appropriate contractual
safeguards.

You can contact **[piotr@remdo.com](mailto:piotr@remdo.com)** for information
about safeguards applicable to transfers of your personal data and, where
applicable, how to obtain a copy of them.

## How long we keep information

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes for which it
is processed.

In particular:

- **Account information and stored content** are generally kept while your
  account or the relevant content exists. If an account or content is deleted,
  it is removed from active processing unless continued retention is required
  for another lawful purpose.
- **Authentication and session information** is kept for as long as needed to
  maintain or secure the relevant session or account.
- **Technical and security logs** are kept for the period needed to troubleshoot
  the service, protect it against abuse and investigate security events.
  Information connected to a specific incident can be retained longer while that
  incident is being investigated or related claims or legal obligations remain
  relevant.
- **Support correspondence and feedback** are kept for as long as needed to
  handle the communication and, where appropriate, maintain a reasonable record
  of the issue.
- **Analytics information**, when analytics is enabled with your consent, is
  retained for a limited period appropriate to analysing product usage. The
  applicable analytics setup and retention period will be described when such
  analytics is enabled.
- **Backup copies** can temporarily contain information that has already been
  deleted from active systems. Such copies disappear as backups are replaced
  according to the applicable backup rotation process.

Information can be retained for longer where required by law or where necessary
to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

## Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal
data.

RemDo is, however, an early-stage service under active development. No online
service can guarantee absolute security, so you should consider the sensitivity
of the information you choose to store in it.

## Automated decision-making

RemDo does not currently use solely automated decision-making, including
profiling, that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly
affects you.

Product experiments such as assigning users to different versions of a feature
do not make decisions of this kind.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to:

- access your personal data;
- correct inaccurate personal data;
- request deletion of your personal data;
- restrict certain processing;
- receive certain data in a portable form;
- object to processing based on legitimate interests;
- withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights or ask a privacy-related question, contact
**[piotr@remdo.com](mailto:piotr@remdo.com)**.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory
authority. In Poland, the supervisory authority is the **President of the
Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych)**.

## Self-hosted RemDo

This Privacy Policy applies to the RemDo service operated at remdo.com.

If you run RemDo yourself or use an instance operated by somebody else, that
operator determines how information on its instance is processed. This Privacy
Policy does not describe the practices of independently operated RemDo
instances.
