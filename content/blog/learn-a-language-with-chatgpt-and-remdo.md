Practise a language with ChatGPT, then save a lesson recap in RemDo with one
short request. The recap records your goals, what went well, what needs more
practice and what to do next. Share the RemDo document with your tutor. They can
see what you practised and add their own notes.

## Keep practising between lessons

You can practise with ChatGPT between lessons with your tutor. Here, the student
asks it to read the previous recap in RemDo. After a quick review, they practise
changing an order at a café.

## Save a recap your tutor can use

At the end, the student writes: “Send recap to the Italian language document in
RemDo.” ChatGPT saves the recap in RemDo. You get useful phrases and corrections
to review, plus a short task to practise before your next lesson.

## Work with your tutor in RemDo

The shared document lets your tutor see what you practised with ChatGPT and
where you needed help. They can add feedback directly and write notes after your
lessons together.

  <img src="https://share.remdo.com/blog/italian-tutor-notes.png"

Before your next session, ask ChatGPT to read your tutor’s notes in RemDo. Try
the exercises your tutor suggested. Then save a new recap for your tutor to
review.

## Make your practice count

With ChatGPT, you can practise a language between lessons. You and your tutor
keep your notes in the same RemDo document. You can see what to practise next,
and your tutor can see where you need help. Bring your questions to the next
lesson and discuss them together.
