An online store can need SEO content for hundreds of product and category pages.
Each page needs relevant keywords, a title and a description. ChatGPT can
prepare drafts in batches, with a template that defines the required fields. As
the catalogue grows, those drafts need a clear structure for review and future
changes. RemDo keeps this content in one place, organised by the categories,
subcategories and products in the store.

## Keep your SEO content together

The RemDo connector lets ChatGPT and other AI assistants save batches of content
directly into your documents. Each product keeps its keywords and SEO copy
together, within the wider store catalogue. You can compare title options, check
descriptions against product details and revise related entries in the same
document. This structure helps keep terms consistent across a product range. The
content remains available between editing sessions, with each draft connected to
the page it belongs to.

## Use a template that fits your website

The template can match the fields used on your website. These can include a
focus keyphrase, SEO title, slug and meta description for Yoast SEO or another
WordPress plugin. After reviewing the copy, you can copy the relevant fields
into the website. RemDo remains the working reference for later changes to
keywords, product details or wording. Separate documents keep each store’s
catalogue and SEO content together.
