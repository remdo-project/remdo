An online store’s SEO content is rarely finished all at once. Some product pages
are ready to update, others need better titles, and another category still needs
descriptions. The person managing this work needs to keep the whole catalogue
consistent while giving each product the right wording. ChatGPT can help prepare
the drafts. Keeping those drafts useful throughout the project takes some
organisation.

<p class="landing-blog-lede">
In our coffee store example, RemDo becomes the working reference for that
content. It is an outliner, so the document follows the store’s categories,
subcategories and products. The person managing SEO can work on an individual
page while keeping its place in the wider catalogue. Keywords, titles and
descriptions stay attached to the products they describe, ready for review or
the next website update.
</p>

## Consistency across the catalogue

Similar products create small decisions that repeat across a store. Which terms
should appear in their titles? Does each description explain the relevant
differences? Does the wording match the product details? Reviewing entries
together makes these decisions easier to compare and helps reveal wording that
needs another look.

For this store, the template contains the four fields needed for Yoast SEO: a
focus keyphrase, SEO title, slug and meta description. We define it in RemDo as
a tree, with Category, Subcategory and Product above the SEO fields. The
connector lets ChatGPT read this structure and save its drafts under the right
products. The same approach can work with other AI assistants that connect to
RemDo. The first video shows how we create the template and ask ChatGPT to use
it for one product.

## The right content when a page needs attention

As more drafts arrive, the document becomes a place to manage the ongoing work.
The person responsible for SEO can review related products together, consider
different title wording and check descriptions against catalogue details. Each
entry contains the fields for one page, with the surrounding category available
for context. This supports both the detailed work on a product and the broader
review of a range.

When a page is ready for an update, its content is already grouped in RemDo. The
person editing the website can use those fields in Yoast SEO, then continue with
the next product. Yoast is the example here, but the template can follow the
requirements of another plugin or website. RemDo holds the working catalogue
throughout that process.

## A starting point for the next update

The value of this catalogue continues after the first website update. Product
details may change, a title may need another version, or a new range may need
content. The person managing SEO can return to the relevant products with the
existing wording and category context already available. For another store, a
separate document can hold its own catalogue and requirements. The next task
starts with the product and the work already prepared for it.
